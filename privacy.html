Privacy Policy — SporeRush: Galaxy Siege

Effective date: 14 November 2026 · Applies to the Android and iOS apps (com.sporerush.galaxysiege)

1. Overview

SporeRush: Galaxy Siege ("the Game", "we", "us") is a single-player mobile game. We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number to play. This policy explains what data the Game stores and which third-party services may process data when you play.

2. Game progress stored on your device

  • Your progress (currencies, upgrades, gear, settings, stars and records) is saved locally on your device using the app's private storage (WebView localStorage). It is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes this progress.
  • The optional Export / Import Save feature creates a text code that contains your save data. The code only leaves your device if you choose to copy and share it yourself.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game offers optional rewarded video ads (for example, combat boosts and bonus production), provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers — the advertising ID (Android AAID / Apple IDFA where you allow tracking) and other device or app instance identifiers;
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address, used to serve relevant ads;
  • App interactions and diagnostics — ad views, taps, performance and crash data.

This data is used for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and analytics. On iOS you will be asked for permission before your IDFA is used, and you can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases

Optional Gem purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing or Apple StoreKit (with RevenueCat used to validate purchases). Your payment details are handled by Google or Apple — no credit card or payment information ever reaches the Game. We receive only an anonymous purchase confirmation and app user ID needed to deliver your Gems and restore purchases.

5. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention & your choices

  • Local game data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the Game.
  • Ad-related data is retained by Google under its own policies; you can limit ad personalization in your device settings at any time.
  • Data sent to third-party services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.
