# 🍄 SporeRush: Galaxy Siege

**Defend your mushroom fortress! Space tower defense meets roguelite mutations.**

The insect swarm is coming, and only one brave Space Mushroom stands between it and the Mycelium Fortress.
SporeRush: Galaxy Siege is a fast arcade action RPG for mobile that mixes one-thumb hero combat, tower defense
and roguelite runs.

- 🍄 **Hero action + tower defense.** Steer the Space Mushroom while your spores auto-fire. Unleash Poison Spore Trip,
  Mycelium Dash, Orbital Spore Barrage and the Mycelium Black Hole, and build Laser, Frost, Mortar and Repair towers on
  your living fortress wall.
- 🧬 **Roguelite mutation drafts.** Slain bugs drop Bio-XP crystals. Fill the bar, draft 1 of 3 mutation cards and stack
  them into wild combos, so every run plays differently.
- 🌌 **120 campaign sectors.** Fight across 12 star clusters, face elite insects and topple the Giant Space Beetle, the
  Void Mantis Empress and the Chitin Centipede Dreadnought.
- 🦸 **Grow your colony.** Evolve your hero, forge gear sets, collect ancient relics, research the Academy tech tree
  and put Mini-Shroom workers to work, even while you're offline.
- ♾️ **Endless Swarm.** See how long you can survive when the waves never stop.
